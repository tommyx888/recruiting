# Recruiting Management System - Manager Guide

## Quick Start

1. **Login** with your email and password
2. You'll see the **Dashboard** with quick action buttons
3. Use the top menu to navigate between sections

---

## What You Can Do

### 1. Create a Job Request

**When**: You need to hire someone for a position

**Steps**:
1. Click **"Create Recruiting Request"** (Dashboard) or **"Create New Request"** (Recruiting Requests page)
2. Fill in:
   - **Department**: Your department (pre-filled, cannot change)
   - **Position**: Select from dropdown
   - **Job Description**: Describe the position
   - **Headcount**: How many people needed
   - **Position Type**: New Position or Replacement
   - **Position Category**: SAL or IND
   - **Reason** (if New Position) or **Name** (if Replacement)
   - **Confidential Request**: Check if needed
3. Click **"Submit Request"**
4. Your request is sent to GM/Recruiter for approval
5. You'll receive an email when it's approved

---

### 2. Add a New Candidate

**When**: You have a candidate to add to the system

**Steps**:
1. Click **"Add New Candidate"** button
2. Fill in:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Department**: Your department (pre-filled)
   - **Position**: Select from dropdown
   - **Source**: Where the candidate came from
   - **Date Obtained**: When you got the candidate
   - **Status**: Usually "New"
   - **Notes**: Any important information
3. Upload **CV File** (required)
4. Upload **Assessment File** (optional)
5. Click **"Add Candidate"**

---

### 3. Manage Candidates

#### View Candidates
- Go to **"Candidates"** in the menu
- You'll see candidates grouped by status
- Use filters to find specific candidates:
  - Department
  - Position
  - Source
  - Status

#### View Candidate Details
- Click **"View Details"** next to any candidate
- See all information, documents, and notes
- Download CV or Assessment
- Re-upload documents if needed

#### Move Candidates Through the Process

**Step 1: Invite to First Round**
- Find candidate with status **"New"**
- Click **"Invite"** button
- A window will appear:
  - ✅ Check **"Notify Recruiter"** to send interview dates
  - Add **interview dates** (date, time, duration in minutes)
  - You can add multiple dates
  - **Optional**: Check **"Specify Interview Type"** → Choose "In Person" or "Teams"
  - **Optional**: Check **"Specify Interviewer"** → Enter interviewer name
- Click **"Continue"** or **"Skip"**
- Status changes to **"In Process - First Round"**

**Step 2: Move to Second Round** (if needed)
- Find candidate with status **"In Process - First Round"**
- Click **"Second Round"** button
- Same window appears - add interview dates if needed
- Click **"Continue"** or **"Skip"**
- Status changes to **"In Process - Second Round"**

**Step 3: Make Decision**

**To Hire**:
- Click **"Hire"** button
- Status: **"Hired - Contact Source"**
- **Important**: Contact the recruitment source
- Then click **"Hired - Source Informed"**
- Final status: **"Hired"**

**To Reject**:
- Click **"Reject"** button
- Enter rejection reason
- Status: **"Rejected - Inform Source"**
- **Important**: Inform the recruitment source
- Then click **"Rejected - Source Informed"**
- Final status: **"Rejected"**

---

### 4. View Your Requests

- Go to **"Recruiting Requests"** in the menu
- See all your requests with their status:
  - **Pending**: Waiting for approval (orange if >3 days, red if >7 days)
  - **Approved**: Ready for recruitment
  - **Rejected**: Not approved
  - **Filled**: Position filled
- Click **"View Details"** to see full information

---

## Interview Dates Feature

When you move a candidate to First or Second Round, you can:

1. **Send dates to recruiters**:
   - Check **"Notify Recruiter"**
   - Add one or more interview dates
   - Recruiters will receive an email with all dates

2. **Add multiple dates**:
   - Click **"Add Interview Date"** button
   - For each date, enter:
     - Date
     - Time
     - Duration (minutes)
   - Click **"Remove"** to delete a date

3. **Optional information**:
   - **Interview Type**: Check box → Choose "In Person" or "Teams"
   - **Interviewer Name**: Check box → Enter name

## Important Notes

✅ **You can**:
- Create requests for your department
- Add candidates
- Update candidate status
- View your department's candidates and requests
- Add interview dates when moving candidates

❌ **You cannot**:
- Approve or reject requests (only GM/Recruiter can)
- See candidates from other departments
- Access GM Approval page
- View statistics or reports

---

## Tips

1. **Always upload CVs** when adding candidates
2. **Add notes** for important information
3. **Use interview dates** to coordinate with recruiters
4. **Keep status updated** as candidates progress
5. **Contact sources** after hiring or rejecting candidates
6. **Check request status** regularly - orange/red highlights mean urgent attention needed
